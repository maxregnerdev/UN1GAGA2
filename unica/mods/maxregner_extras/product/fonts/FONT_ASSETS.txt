# Maxregner Sans — Font Assets

The Maxregner Sans typeface is a binary asset and is not checked in here.
Drop the TrueType binary into this folder:

    product/fonts/maxregner_sans.ttf

Recommended: a geometric sans-serif with a full Latin + extended set,
hinted, weights 400/500/600/700 normalized. The `maxregner_sans.xml`
family entry next to it points OneUI's font picker at this file.
